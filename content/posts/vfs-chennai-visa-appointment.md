## Booking the slot

Slots at VFS Chennai filled up faster than I expected once my blocked account
and health insurance were sorted. I'd read that checking right after midnight
was the move, and — for whatever it's worth — that's when a slot actually
opened up for me too.

## The day of

I got there well before my appointment time, mostly out of nerves. The queue
outside moved faster than the one inside; document verification took longer
than the biometrics step itself. Every folder ahead of me in line seemed to
be missing exactly one paper, which made the wait feel longer than it
probably was.

## What I over-prepared for

I'd printed extra copies of nearly everything — bank statements, the
admission letter, insurance proof — most of which nobody asked to see twice.
The one thing that actually got double-checked closely was the blocked
account confirmation, so if there's a single document worth triple-checking
before you go, that would be it.

## After

Once biometrics and document submission were done, there wasn't much to do
but wait for the passport to come back. That part, at least, was quieter
than the appointment itself.
